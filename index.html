HOW TO PLAY

1. MOVE & RECOVER

WASD to move, Space to jump, Shift to dash. You can air-steer & double-jump. Near the edge? Dash toward center to survive!

2. ATTACK & LAUNCH

LMB = Quick (fast, low KB). RMB = Heavy (slow, huge KB). Damage % increases knockback. Launch foes off the island!

3. ABILITY & POWERUPS

Press E for Viber ability. Grab floating cubes: ⚡ SPEED, 💥 POWER, 🛡 SHIELD. Last Viber standing wins.
